## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Local Pickup Only

Rackz is one of our two **local-only** buses (the other is Skittles). It runs in:

- Houma
- Thibodaux
- Terrebonne and Lafourche parishes
- Areas within roughly a 45-minute drive of Houma

**Rackz does NOT travel to** New Orleans, Metairie, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, Lake
Charles, the Northshore, or anywhere requiring more than ~45 minutes of driving each
way.

If a customer is outside the Houma area and wants Rackz, redirect them to **Orange
Juice ($135/hr)** or **Kuttin Loose ($125/hr)** — same 40-passenger capacity, no
hourly minimum, wide travel range.

## What Makes Rackz Special

Rackz is the **VIP statement bus** of the fleet. Sleek maroon exterior. Built for
nights where the customer wants the bus to *say something* about how they're rolling.

Common booking pattern: a local Houma or Thibodaux customer planning a 30th or 40th
birthday, a high-end bachelor party, or a "make it rain" celebration. They want VIP
energy without leaving the local area.

The 3-hour minimum makes the math work for local pricing — short runs cover the
driver and operating cost, but the lower hourly rate ($120 vs. $135 for Orange Juice)
means more budget left over for the rest of the night.

## Common Customer Questions

**"Why is there a minimum?"**
> Rackz is priced for local nights — the 3-hour minimum keeps the math working at $120/hr instead of marking up the per-hour rate. Once you hit 3 hours, every additional hour stays at $120. No long-distance travel, which keeps the rate lower.

**"Can it come to New Orleans?"**
> Rackz stays local to the Houma area. For New Orleans, Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose would be the move — wider travel, no minimum. Same 40-passenger capacity. Want pics of those?

**"What's included for $120/hr?"**
> Bus, driver, fuel, ice and water, climate control, sound system, lights. No hidden fees.

**"What's the minimum total?"**
> $120 × 3 hours = $360 minimum for the night.

**"Can we go to Thibodaux?"**
> Absolutely — Thibodaux is in the local zone. Anywhere in Terrebonne or Lafourche parish works.

**"What if I only need 2 hours?"**
> The 3-hour minimum applies for Rackz. If you only need 2 hours, Cotton Candy at $110/hr (no minimum, $220 for 2 hours) would actually be cheaper. Want to compare?
