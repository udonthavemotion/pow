# Event Types — Playbook For Common Use Cases

How to talk to customers based on the kind of event they're planning. Each section
includes the right bus to recommend, common questions, and the pitch.

---

## Bachelor & Bachelorette Parties (#1 Use Case)

This is the single most common booking category at Party On Wheels.

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Bachelorette, photo-heavy, cute vibe** → Cotton Candy
- **Bachelorette, wild night** → Kuttin Loose
- **Bachelorette local-only, club energy** → Skittles *(when active)*
- **Bachelor, high energy, max sound** → Kuttin Loose
- **Bachelor, VIP local** → Rackz
- **Either, doing it big with parade-energy in the Quarter** → Orange Juice

**Typical booking:** 5–8 hours, weekend night, often starting with pre-game at the
house and ending at a bar / club / dinner spot.

**Common questions:**

*"Can we go bar-to-bar in the Quarter?"*
> Absolutely — that's the most common bachelorette plan. Driver knows the Quarter and
> can wait outside while y'all stop in. Pick a wide-travel bus (Orange Juice, Kuttin
> Loose, or Cotton Candy).

*"We have decorations / a sash / banners — can we bring them on?"*
> Yes. Bring whatever you want. Driver can help set up basic stuff before pickup.

*"How many girls / guys?"*
> All our buses fit 40, so unless you're going over that, you're good. How many in the crew?

**Pitch line:**
> "Bachelorettes are our biggest booking — three buses fit the vibe depending on what
> you want the night to look like. Cute and photo-heavy? Cotton Candy. Going hard?
> Kuttin Loose. Want both? Tell me about the crew."

## Birthday Parties

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **21st birthday, going to the Quarter** → Kuttin Loose or Orange Juice
- **Sweet 16 / Quinceañera** → Cotton Candy
- **30th / 40th / 50th milestone, local** → Rackz (premium feel)
- **30th / 40th / 50th milestone, traveling** → Orange Juice or Cotton Candy
- **Kid's birthday / family-friendly** → Cotton Candy (Deric to confirm alcohol-free policy)

**Typical booking:** 4–6 hours, evening, often pickup at the house or restaurant.

**Common questions:**

*"What do you put on the bus — any birthday decorations?"*
> The buses come dressed-up by default (lights, vibes). We don't add custom banners
> or balloons — bring your own and we'll make sure there's room.

*"Can we sing happy birthday on the bus?"*
> Of course. Loud as you want.

**Pitch line:**
> "Birthdays are huge for us — what's the year [21st, 30th, 40th] and is this a
> stay-local night or are y'all heading to the Quarter / Baton Rouge / somewhere?"

## Mardi Gras (Massive Season)

Mardi Gras is the busiest time of year. The bus becomes the parade base. Bathrooms,
AC, a place to escape the crowd between throws, and sound for the whole crew.

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Parade route in Houma or Thibodaux** → Any bus — Orange Juice for max presence
- **Parade route in New Orleans (Uptown, Mid-City, French Quarter)** → Orange Juice (open-air back deck is parade gold), Kuttin Loose, or Cotton Candy
- **Multi-day Mardi Gras weekend** → 10–12 hours/day, the more travel-friendly the better → Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose

**Important:**
- Mardi Gras week books up **weeks in advance**. Push customers to book early.
- Some parade-day bookings have higher hourly windows (typically 8–12 hours) — confirm with the customer.
- Local-only buses (Rackz, Skittles) work fine for Houma parades but not New Orleans parade routes.

**Common questions:**

*"Can you drop us at a parade route and pick us back up?"*
> Yes — most Mardi Gras bookings work like that. Driver stays close, you party the
> route, hop back on when you need a break.

*"Can we throw beads off the bus?"*
> If the bus is parked and stationary, sure. While moving, no throws — safety thing.
> Orange Juice's open-air deck is built for this — the move for parades.

*"How early should I book for Fat Tuesday?"*
> ASAP — 4–6 weeks minimum, ideally earlier. Mardi Gras is our busiest day.

**Pitch line:**
> "Mardi Gras is our biggest weekend — what parish are y'all riding in, and which
> days? Want to lock the bus in now before they all go?"

## Saints Game Days (NFL) / LSU Game Days

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Saints game in New Orleans** → Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose
- **LSU game in Baton Rouge** → Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose
- **Pelicans game at Smoothie King** → Cotton Candy or Kuttin Loose

**Typical booking:** 6–10 hours, daytime start, pickup at home or a tailgate spot,
drop-off after the game or after-party.

**Common questions:**

*"Can y'all tailgate with us at the Dome?"*
> Driver doesn't tailgate but the bus stays parked nearby — you can use it as base.
> Orange Juice's open-air back deck is gold for tailgates.

*"Can we drink during the game and have you take us home?"*
> That's the whole point of booking us — nobody drives. Drop you at the Dome, pick
> you back up after, get y'all home safe.

**Pitch line:**
> "Game day's our specialty — Saints or LSU? Pickup spot? I'll line you up with the
> right bus."

## Weddings & Wedding Shuttles

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Wedding party shuttle (bridal party only, day-of)** → Cotton Candy or Orange Juice
- **Guest shuttle from hotel to venue** → Any wide-travel bus (multiple bookings often)
- **Bachelorette night for the bride** → Cotton Candy or Kuttin Loose
- **Post-reception ride for the bride and groom** → Cotton Candy (photo-friendly exit)

**Typical booking:** 3–6 hours, daytime or evening, often split into two windows
(pre-reception shuttle + post-reception shuttle).

**Common questions:**

*"Can the bus go between the church and the reception?"*
> Yes — that's a common wedding setup. Send us the addresses at the calendar checkout.

*"Do you do guest shuttles or just the wedding party?"*
> Both. For guest shuttles you'd typically book a bus to run hotel-to-venue
> back-and-forth — let us know how many guests and the timing.

*"Can the bride and groom ride alone in the back?"*
> Whatever y'all want.

**Pitch line:**
> "Weddings are big for us — are we talking wedding-party shuttle, guest shuttle, or
> post-reception ride? Once I know the role, I can recommend the right bus."

## Nights Out (Bar / Club / Restaurant Hopping)

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Local Houma night** → Rackz or Cotton Candy
- **Quarter night in New Orleans** → Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, or Cotton Candy
- **Going out to multiple stops** → Wide-travel buses

**Typical booking:** 4–6 hours, evening start, multiple stops, drop home at end of night.

**Pitch line:**
> "Night out in [city] — how many in the crew, and are we talking 4 hours or longer?"

## Casino Runs (Houma / Baton Rouge / Lake Charles)

**Default bus recommendations:**
- Local casinos (Houma area) → Rackz
- Coushatta / Cypress Bayou / longer drives → Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose

**Typical booking:** 6–10 hours, evening start, casino → return.

**Important:** Long casino runs to Lake Charles or further should be confirmed with
Deric — travel hours factor into the quote.

## Concert & Festival Rides

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Jazz Fest, Essence, New Orleans concerts** → Kuttin Loose or Orange Juice
- **Local Houma concerts** → any bus
- **Festival camping ride** → Hand off to Deric for multi-day pricing

**Typical booking:** 4–6 hours, often coordinated with show start/end times.

## Corporate / Holiday Parties

**Default bus recommendations:**
- **Holiday party shuttle for staff** → Cotton Candy or Kuttin Loose
- **Executive / VIP corporate** → Rackz (if local) or Orange Juice

**Typical booking:** 3–5 hours, evening, pickup at office and drop at venue.

**Important:** Some corporate clients want invoicing instead of card-at-checkout — hand off to Deric for those.

## Prom & Homecoming

**Default bus recommendations:**
- Cotton Candy (photogenic, parent-approved vibe) or Orange Juice

**Important rules:**
- **Adult chaperone required** for any prom / homecoming booking.
- **No alcohol** when underage passengers are on board — strict rule.
- Deric to confirm any prom booking before final.
