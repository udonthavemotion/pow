## Pricing & Quotes

### Q: How much is it?
A: Hourly rates run from $100/hr (Skittles, local-only, currently offline) up to $135/hr (Orange Juice, flagship). What bus you eyeing and how many hours?

### Q: What's your cheapest bus?
A: When Skittles is back, it's $100/hr (local only, 3-hr minimum). Right now your cheapest options are **Cotton Candy at $110/hr** (no minimum, travels anywhere in South Louisiana) or **Rackz at $120/hr** (local only, 3-hr minimum). Cotton Candy is the best value bus we have.

### Q: What's your most expensive bus?
A: Orange Juice — $135/hr. The flagship with the open-air back deck.

### Q: Why does Rackz have a minimum but Cotton Candy doesn't?
A: Rackz and Skittles are our two local-only buses with lower hourly rates. The 3-hour minimum keeps the math working at those rates. The wide-travel buses (Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, Cotton Candy) have no minimum.

### Q: Is there a fuel surcharge?
A: Nope. Hourly rate covers fuel within the service area. Long-distance trips can have additional travel hours but no junk fees.

### Q: What's included in the rate?
A: Bus, licensed driver, fuel, climate control, premium sound, LED lights, ice and water onboard. No surprise fees.

### Q: Do you take card?
A: Yes — everything goes through the booking calendar, card on file. We don't take cash deals.

### Q: How much is the deposit?
A: The calendar shows the deposit at checkout — it's small enough to lock the date. Want me to send the link?

### Q: Can I get a discount?
A: Posted rate is the rate — Deric keeps it clean so the customer always knows what they're paying. If you want to talk specifics, I can have him reach out — what's the best number?

### Q: Do you charge more for Mardi Gras?
A: Same hourly rate as any other day. The catch is **availability** — Mardi Gras books up weeks ahead.
