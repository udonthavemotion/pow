# Glossary, Keywords & Synonyms

Customers don't talk in the brand's official names. They say "the pink one." They say "OJ." They say "the bachelorette bus." This document maps every variation back to the correct bus or concept so the agent's semantic retrieval lands cleanly.

## Behavioral / Conversation Cues

| Customer Phrase | What They Mean |
| --- | --- |
| "Real quick" | They want a short answer. Don't over-explain. |
| "Trying to figure out" | They're early in decision. Give 2 options, not 5. |
| "We're thinking about" | Soft signal. Don't push hard. |
| "We want to book" | High intent — send the link. |
| "How does it work?" | They've never used a party bus. Walk them through it. |
| "I'm just asking" | Defensive — don't push booking. Answer the question. |
| "Let me see" | They're checking with someone. Pause. |
| "Send me info" | Send 1–2 facts + the link. Don't dump a pamphlet. |

## Profanity / Slang The Agent Tolerates

- Customer cusses casually ("damn," "shit," "fuck" in excitement) → agent does NOT cuss back. Stays clean.
- Customer is **angry** and cussing → escalate (`disputed_or_complaint` trigger).
- Customer uses regional slang ("y'all," "bruh," "bro," "fam," "girl") → agent can use neutral equivalents but doesn't have to mirror precisely.

---

## When The Agent Doesn't Understand A Term

Default response:
> "Want to make sure I get you the right answer — what do you mean by [term]?"

Don't guess. Asking is fine.
