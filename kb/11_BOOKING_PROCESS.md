# Booking Process — How Customers Actually Lock In A Date

The agent's job is to get the customer onto the **right booking calendar** for their bus. That's where everything else happens: date hold, contact info, deposit.

---

## The 5-Step Booking Flow

1. **Agent helps customer pick a bus** (using the fleet guide).
2. **Agent confirms date and approximate hours.**
3. **Agent confirms pickup city / area** (so the right bus is even eligible).
4. **Agent sends the direct booking calendar link** for that bus.
5. **Customer fills out the calendar form and pays the deposit.** Booking confirmed.

The agent does NOT take dates, names, or payment directly in chat. The calendar handles all of it.

## How To Send The Calendar Link (Script)

After the customer says they want a specific bus and gives you the date and hours, send something like:

> "Got it — [bus name] for [date], around [X] hours. Here's the direct link to lock it in: [URL]. Pick your time, fill out the quick form, and you're set. Holler if you hit any weird snags."

That's all the agent needs to do. Once the link is sent, the customer takes it from there.

---

## What Information The Calendar Will Ask For

The customer will fill in:
- First and last name
- Email
- Phone
- Pickup location
- Event type (Mardi Gras, wedding, birthday, bachelorette, etc.)
- Number of guests
- Special requests
- Date/time
- Deposit / payment

The agent does **not** need to collect any of this in advance. The calendar handles it.

---

## Picking The Right Calendar When Customer Hasn't Committed Yet

If the customer is browsing and hasn't picked a bus:
- Don't dump all 5 links. Pick the top 1–2 best matches and send those.
- Example: bachelorette in Houma → send Cotton Candy calendar. *"Cotton Candy is our bachelorette go-to — here's the calendar to lock it in."*
- If the customer says "let me think," do NOT keep pushing. Reply: *"Sounds good — link's right here when you're ready: [URL]. Let me know if you have questions."*

---

## Time Slots & Availability

- Calendars show real-time availability — the agent does NOT manually check or
  promise dates.
- If a customer asks "is [date] available?", say: *"The calendar will show you availability in real time — here's the link: [URL]. If something looks blocked and you want to double-check, let me know."*
- If the customer reports the calendar shows no slots on their date, hand off to Deric.

## Mardi Gras Booking Window

- Mardi Gras week is the busiest period of the year.
- Customers should book **at least 4–6 weeks in advance** for parade-day rides.
- The agent should proactively flag this when a customer mentions Mardi Gras: *"Heads up, Mardi Gras week books up fast — let's lock the bus in now."*

---

## Wedding Booking Window

- Weddings should be booked **at least 3–6 weeks out**.
- For wedding shuttles, customers often book 2–3 months in advance.
- Confirm with the customer:
  - **Pickup point** (church, venue, hotel?)
  - **Drop-off point** (reception, after-party, hotel?)
  - **Total hours needed** (sometimes split — pre-reception and post-reception)

---

## Deposit & Payment Info (For The Agent)

The agent does NOT quote deposit amounts. Deposits are handled at the calendar checkout.

If asked, the agent says:
> "The calendar will show you the deposit when you check out — it's small enough to lock the date and confirms the booking. Want the link?"

---

## After Booking Is Confirmed (What Happens Next)

After the customer books:
1. They get an automated confirmation (email + SMS).
2. The contact appears in Deric's GHL inbox with the bus tag (`bus-orange-juice`, etc.) and the event-type tag.
3. They move to the **"Booking Confirmed"** pipeline stage.
4. 48 hours before the event, an automated reminder goes out.
5. Day-of, the driver confirms pickup.

The agent doesn't manage any of this. The GHL workflow handles it once the calendar booking fires.

## Common Booking Questions

**"How far in advance should I book?"**
> For most events, 2–3 weeks. For Mardi Gras and big wedding weekends, 4–6 weeks or more.

**"Can I change the date later?"**
> Date changes depend on availability — easiest path is to message us back through this number and we'll get Deric to adjust it.

**"What's the cancellation policy?"**
> Cancellation details are in the booking calendar's terms — let me get Deric to confirm specifics for your case. What's the best number to text you back?

**"Can I split the bus across two pickups?"**
> Usually yes for nearby pickups. Best to flag that in the special-requests field at the calendar so the driver plans the route.

**"What if I want a different bus than what's available on my date?"**
> Try the other calendars — different buses are on different schedules. If everything's booked, we can put you on a waitlist.

---

## What The Agent Will Never Do During Booking

- **Never** confirm a booking without sending the customer to the calendar.
- **Never** collect card or payment details in chat.
- **Never** override the calendar's availability ("I'll squeeze you in" is not a thing).
- **Never** promise a custom route or driver request without owner approval — collect the request, send it to Deric.
