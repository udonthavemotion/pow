## What POW Does (In Plain English)

Party On Wheels rents out party buses — actual rolling parties on wheels. Customers
book a bus, a driver, and a window of time. We show up, pick them up, drive them
wherever they want to go that night (within the service area), and bring them home
safe.

Every bus comes with:
- A licensed, sober driver (you do not drive yourself)
- Premium sound system the customer can plug into
- LED light show inside
- Climate control (AC + heat)
- Onboard coolers (most buses) with ice and water
- Comfortable seating for up to 40 people (limo seats up to 20)

## Who Books POW

- **Mardi Gras parades** — rolling parade base with a bathroom, sound, and AC

- **Corporate events, holiday parties, casino runs**

## Why Customers Pick POW Over Other Bus Companies

## Service Tier Quick-Read

POW has **two service tiers** based on the bus:

**Tier 1 — Wide Travel** (Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, Cotton Candy): higher hourly
rates, no hourly minimums, travel anywhere in South Louisiana including New
Orleans, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, and surrounding cities for the right job.

**Tier 2 — Local Only** (Rackz, Skittles): lower hourly rates with a 3-hour
minimum. **Houma, Thibodaux, and surrounding area only.** These buses don't make
long drives to the Quarter or Baton Rouge — they're built for local nights out.
