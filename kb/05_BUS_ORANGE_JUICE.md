## What Makes Orange Juice Special

This is the **flagship**. If you've seen a Party On Wheels bus around Houma or rolling
through the Quarter, odds are good it was Orange Juice. It's the bus people point at
in traffic. It's the one in everyone's Instagram stories.

The signature feature is the **open-air back deck** — the rear of the bus has an open-
air party platform you can step out onto while the bus is parked. Nothing else in the
fleet has it. For parade routes, tailgates, and photo moments, it's the move.

## Key Features

- **Open-air back deck** (exclusive to Orange Juice)
- Wrap-around seating for the whole crew
- Premium sound system — plug in your playlist, hit play
- LED light show inside
- Onboard coolers
- Ice and water provided
- Climate control
- High capacity (40 passengers)
- Iconic orange exterior — it's the "main character" bus

## Best For

- **Saints game day tailgates** — easy to find in the lot, room for the whole crew
- **Big-energy birthdays** (especially 30/40/50 milestones)
- **Anyone who wants the most "wow" reaction when the bus pulls up**
- **Photo-heavy events** — the bus itself is half the photo

## When NOT To Recommend Orange Juice

- Small groups under 12 — they'll be lost in the space
- Customers who specifically want a "cute" / pastel vibe — that's Cotton Candy
- Customers who want a pole / dance floor — Skittles is the answer there (but Skittles is currently unavailable)
- Customers focused on price over presence — Cotton Candy or Rackz are cheaper options

## Common Customer Questions

**"How does the open-air deck work?"**
> Open-air platform off the back of the bus. You can step out onto it when the bus is parked — perfect for Mardi Gras throws, tailgate photos, or just stepping outside without missing the party. Driver keeps it gated and safe while moving.

**"Is it the biggest one?"**
> All five of our buses seat 40. Orange Juice has the biggest visual impact, but capacity-wise it's tied with the rest of the fleet.

**"Can we use it for a Saints game?"**
> Absolutely — Orange Juice runs to the Dome. The open-air deck is great for the tailgate. Just let us know what time you want to roll out from Houma (or wherever you're picking up from) and where you want the night to end.

**"How much for 6 hours?"**
> $135 × 6 = $810 for the bus and driver. Fuel, ice, and water included. Where's the event?
