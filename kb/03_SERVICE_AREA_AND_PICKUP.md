# Service Area & Pickup Rules

This is one of the most important documents in the knowledge base. **Pickup location rules vary by bus.** Get this right every time.

---

## Headquarters

Party On Wheels is based in **Houma, Louisiana** (102 Clendenning Rd, Houma, LA 70363). Every bus departs from and returns to Houma at the end of the night.

---

## The Two-Tier Service Model

POW operates two tiers of service. Which tier a bus falls into is determined by its **hourly minimum** — if a bus has a minimum, it's local only.

### Tier 1 — Wide Travel (no hourly minimum)
These buses can travel for the right job. Pickup can be in Houma, Thibodaux, New Orleans, Lafayette, Baton Rouge, Lake Charles, or anywhere reasonable in South Louisiana.

- **Orange Juice** — $135/hr
- **Kuttin Loose** — $125/hr
- **Cotton Candy** — $110/hr

These are the buses for:
- French Quarter nights
- Saints / Pelicans games at the Superdome / Smoothie King Center
- Baton Rouge LSU game days
- Lafayette / Cajun country festivals
- Out-of-area weddings
- Anything more than ~45 minutes from Houma

### Tier 2 — Local Pickup Only
**Rackz** and **Skittles** are local-only buses. They have a **3-hour minimum** and only operate in:

- **Houma**
- **Thibodaux**
- **Surrounding parishes** — Terrebonne, Lafourche, and adjacent areas
- **Anywhere within a reasonable drive of Houma** (roughly 30–45 minutes max)

These buses do **NOT** travel to:
- New Orleans / Metairie / Kenner
- Baton Rouge
- Lafayette
- Lake Charles
- Slidell or the Northshore
- Anywhere requiring more than about a 45-minute drive each way

**Why:** Rackz and Skittles have lower hourly rates with a minimum book. The pricing
works for local nights out where the driver can stay close to base. For longer drives,
the math only works on the wide-travel buses.

## How To Answer "Can You Pick Us Up In [city]?"

### Customer in Houma, Thibodaux, Terrebonne Parish, or Lafourche Parish
**Any bus is fine.** Recommend based on event vibe, not geography.

### Customer in New Orleans, Metairie, Kenner, the Northshore
**Tier 1 only** — Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, or Cotton Candy.
> "We absolutely run to the Quarter — Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, and Cotton Candy all travel up that way. Rackz and Skittles stay local to Houma, but you've got three solid options."

### Customer in Baton Rouge, Lafayette, or further
**Tier 1 only.** Confirm pickup location and add it to the booking note. Travel time will count toward the hourly rate (door-to-door from Houma to the pickup city and back).

### Customer in Mississippi, Alabama, or out of state
**Hand off to Deric.** Out-of-state runs require special insurance review and case-by-case approval.

## Travel Time & Hourly Billing

For wide-travel jobs:
- The hourly clock typically starts when the driver arrives at the pickup location.
- Some long-distance trips include drive time from Houma — depends on the job.
- For exact billing on out-of-area trips, the agent should send the customer to the booking calendar and let Deric confirm the final quote.

For local jobs:
- The hourly clock typically starts at the scheduled pickup time, at the customer's pickup location, within the service area.

## Common Customer Phrases & The Right Answer

**"Y'all come to New Orleans?"**
> "Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, and Cotton Candy run to New Orleans no problem. Rackz and Skittles stay local to Houma. What's the event?"

**"Can the cheap one come pick us up in Metairie?"**
> "The local buses (Rackz, Skittles) stay around Houma. For Metairie, the Cotton Candy is our most affordable travel-friendly bus at $110/hr — still 40-passenger. Want to see it?"

**"How far will y'all drive?"**
> "For our wide-travel buses (Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, Cotton Candy), pretty much anywhere in South Louisiana — Houma, Thibodaux, New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Lafayette. The local buses stay around Houma. Where you trying to roll?"

**"We're in Lafayette, can you make it?"**
> "Yes — Orange Juice, Kuttin Loose, or Cotton Candy can run to Lafayette. Travel time gets factored into the hours so let's pick a bus and I'll get you on the right calendar so Deric can confirm the exact quote."

**"Can Rackz come to a wedding in Mandeville?"**
> "Rackz is local-only to the Houma area, but the same VIP feel is available with Orange Juice or Kuttin Loose for that distance. Want me to send pics?"

## What "Reasonable Area" Means For Tier 2

If a customer asks for Rackz or Skittles and is borderline (e.g., Morgan City, Raceland), the rule of thumb is:
- **Under 45 minutes drive from Houma each way: usually OK** — confirm with Deric.
- **Over 45 minutes: too far for the local-tier pricing.** Offer Tier 1 instead.

When in doubt, default to handing off to Deric: *"Let me confirm with Deric since you're right on the edge of the local zone — what's the best number to text you back?"*

---

## Why This Matters

A customer in Baton Rouge who's quoted Rackz at $120/hr is being set up to be disappointed when they find out it's local only. **Get the bus-area match right at the start of the conversation** and the booking goes smoothly.
