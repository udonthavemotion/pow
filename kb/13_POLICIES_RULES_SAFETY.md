# Policies, Rules & Safety

What's allowed, what's not, and how the agent should handle each common question.

---

## Alcohol Policy

- **Allowed:** Yes — customers can bring their own alcohol on board for adult bookings.
- **NOT provided:** POW does not sell, serve, or provide alcohol.
- **Underage:** Strict no-alcohol policy if any passenger is under 21.
- **Excessive intoxication:** Driver has discretion to end the ride if any passenger is dangerously intoxicated, belligerent, or jeopardizing safety. No refund in that case.
- **Open containers in the bus:** Allowed while bus is moving (party bus exception under LA law for licensed party-bus transportation).

**Agent script for "Can we bring alcohol?":**
> "Yep — bring your own. We don't provide it but the bus is BYO. Driver has cooler space if you need ice."

---

## Smoking, Vaping, Open Flames

- **NO smoking, vaping, or open flames inside the bus.** Strict.
- **Smoke breaks at stops:** Fine, when the bus is parked.

**Agent script:**
> "No smoking or vaping inside — keeps the bus fresh for everyone. Smoke breaks at stops are no problem."

---

## Food On Board

- **Allowed:** Yes — snacks, takeout, light food are all fine.
- **NOT recommended:** Anything saucy, greasy, or messy enough to stain the seats — common sense rule.
- **No cooking** on the bus (no Sterno, no chafing dishes, no flames).

**Agent script:**
> "Food's fine — bring whatever. Just nothing that's going to stain or set off a fire alarm. Be cool with the next crew that books."

---

## Cleanliness & Damage

- The bus arrives clean. Customer is expected to return it in similar condition.
- **Excessive mess** (vomit, broken bottles, spilled drinks all over seats) may
  trigger a **cleaning fee** — typically $150–$300 depending on severity. Deric makes
  the final call.
- **Damage to the bus** (broken seats, broken sound equipment, pole damage on
  Skittles, etc.) is charged at repair cost. Card on file may be used.

**Agent script if asked about fees:**
> "Standard mess and confetti? No worries. Real damage or vomit cleanup is where a
> cleaning fee kicks in — Deric makes that call case-by-case."

---

## Number Of Passengers

- **Max capacity:** 40 on every party bus.
- **The driver does not seat-count passengers** unless it looks unsafe.
- **Standing while moving:** Customer's call — they're adults — but seatbelts are
  available if customers want them.

**Agent script:**
> "All five buses fit 40 — if you're at or under that, you're good."

---

## Pets / Service Animals

- **Service animals:** Welcome, no restriction.
- **Pets / non-service animals:** Generally not allowed unless pre-approved by Deric.

**Agent script:**
> "Service animals are always welcome. For pets, let me check with Deric — what kind of animal?"

---

## Minors / Underage Passengers

- **Allowed with chaperone:** Yes, for proms, homecomings, sweet sixteens, school events.
- **No alcohol** if any passenger is under 21.
- **Adult chaperone required** for any booking primarily for minors.
- Bookings with minors need owner sign-off.

**Agent script for prom/homecoming:**
> "We do proms and homecomings — needs an adult chaperone on the bus, and no alcohol
> if anyone's under 21. Let me get Deric to confirm the booking once you've got the
> details locked."

---

## Music & Volume

- Customer controls the music — plug in via Bluetooth or AUX.
- Volume can go loud, but driver has authority to ask for it down if it's a safety issue (couldn't hear directions, etc.)
- No live performances / DJs on bus unless pre-approved.

---

## Stops & Route Changes

- Customer can request stops during the ride.
- Reasonable route changes are fine.
- Major route changes (going to a different city than booked) may require approval.

**Agent script:**
> "You can hop off at stops along the way — let the driver know your plan. Big route changes (going to a different city than what you booked) might need to clear with us first."

---

## Late Pickups & Running Behind

- If the customer is running late at pickup, the hourly clock still typically starts at the scheduled time.
- If the driver is running late, POW covers the extra time at no extra cost.

**Agent script:**
> "If y'all are running late on pickup, the clock keeps going from your scheduled start. If we're late, we eat that time."

---

## Weather / Cancellations

- POW operates rain or shine.
- Severe weather (hurricanes, tropical storms, ice warnings) may pause operations — Deric handles refunds / reschedules for those.
- Customer cancellations vary by how close to the event — calendar / Deric handles specifics.

**Agent script for hurricane / severe weather:**
> "If a storm shuts us down, Deric handles the refund or reschedule personally. I'll
> ping him for you — what's the booking name on the calendar?"

---

## Tipping The Driver

- **Not required, appreciated.**
- 15–20% of the rate is the typical tip range for happy customers.

**Agent script:**
> "Tip's the customer's call — most folks who had a great night drop the driver
> 15–20%. Not required, just appreciated."

---

## Safety Rules (Driver-Enforced)

- Driver can end the ride for: fighting, weapons, illegal drugs, throwing objects from a moving bus, behavior that endangers anyone on board, or anything illegal.
- No refund if the ride is ended for safety violations.

**Agent script if asked about house rules:**
> "Driver's the boss for safety stuff — fights, weapons, anything illegal ends the
> ride immediately, no refund. Everything else, we keep the party rolling."

---

## Insurance & Licensing

- All buses are insured.
- All drivers are licensed and DOT-compliant.
- POW carries commercial transportation insurance.

**Agent script if asked:**
> "We're fully insured and DOT-compliant — every driver is licensed for commercial passenger transport. Send any insurance certificate requests through the booking calendar and Deric will handle it."

---

## Privacy & Recording

- POW does **NOT** record audio/video inside the bus.
- Customers can record themselves freely.

---

## How To Handle "Is It Safe?" Questions

When a parent / cautious customer asks safety-related questions:
> "Totally fair question — every driver is licensed and sober the entire ride, the
> buses are commercial-insured, and we follow DOT rules. The whole point is so nobody
> in the crew has to drive."

---

## How To Handle Customer Disputes

If a customer is upset about a charge, a delay, or a driver issue:
**Hand off to Deric immediately.** Do not try to resolve disputes in chat.
> "I want Deric on this directly — what's the best number to call you back, and
> what's the booking name?"

Tag the conversation with `needs-owner-callback` and `disputed`.
