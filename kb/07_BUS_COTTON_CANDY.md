## What Makes Cotton Candy Special

Cotton Candy is the **photo bus**. Every other party bus in Louisiana is some
variation of black, chrome, or LED purple. Cotton Candy is pink and blue, festive,
and looks like a celebration before anyone even steps on.

It's the bus you book when the photos matter as much as the night. Bachelorette
parties. Sweet sixteens. Big birthdays where everyone's in matching outfits and the
group chat is already planning the Instagram dump.

It's also our **best-value wide-travel bus** at $110/hr — cheaper than Orange Juice
and Kuttin Loose, same 40-passenger capacity, same wide travel range.

## When NOT To Recommend Cotton Candy

- Customer wants the most "wow" exterior reaction — that's Orange Juice
- Customer wants max loudness and dance-floor energy — that's Kuttin Loose
- Customer wants a pole/dance floor specifically — that's Skittles (currently unavailable)
- Customer wants a "masculine" / VIP statement bus — that's Rackz (local only)
