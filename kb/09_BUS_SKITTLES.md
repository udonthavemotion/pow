# Bus Profile — Skittles

## Quick Facts

- **Name:** Skittles (formerly known as "Dirty Dancer")
- **Tagline:** *Taste the rainbow on wheels.*
- **Hourly rate:** $100/hour
- **Capacity:** Up to 40 passengers
- **Minimum hours:** **3-hour minimum**
- **Travel range:** **LOCAL ONLY — Houma, Thibodaux, and surrounding parishes**
- **Availability:** 🚧 **Currently being refreshed — NOT bookable right now**
- **Color / look:** Multi-color interior with club-quality lighting
- **Customer nicknames:** "The Rainbow Bus," "The Pole Bus," "Dirty Dancer," "The Dance Bus"
- **Media Library file:** `Skittles — hero.jpg`
- **Direct booking calendar:** [Skittles Booking](https://link.zeromotionmarketing.com/rentals/party-on-wheels-/rl/69a78d2edcc1c11d37b7385d) — *not currently accepting bookings*

## What Makes Skittles Special (When Active)

Skittles is the **club on wheels**. The signature features are a **dance pole** and a
**dedicated dance floor** — no other bus in the fleet has either one. It's the bus a
customer books when the night plan is *"the bus is the destination."* Roll around
Houma, blast the sound, take turns on the pole, dance the whole way.

At $100/hr it's also our **lowest hourly rate**, with a 3-hour minimum to make the
local-pricing math work.

## Key Features (When Active)

- **Dance pole** (exclusive feature)
- **Dedicated dance floor**
- **Club-quality sound system** — built for movement
- Premium club-style lighting
- Climate control
