The sound system is dialed up. The lights are turned up. The interior is built for a

- Quiet rehearsal dinners or older crowds
- Photo-focused events that want a soft / cute aesthetic (Cotton Candy)
- Customers who want the open-air back deck (that's Orange Juice)
- Small intimate groups under 10 — they'll get swallowed by the space

## Common Customer Questions

**"How loud is it?"**
> Loud. Premium sound system tuned for a moving dance floor. You can also dial it down if anyone in the crew wants to talk — but it can go loud.

**"Can we play our own music?"**
> Yes. Plug in via Bluetooth or AUX. Bring your playlist, hit play, you're in control.

**"Is there a dance floor?"**
> Open floor space designed for moving. Not a wood dance floor like Skittles, but plenty of room to dance.

**"How does it compare to Orange Juice?"**
> Same capacity (40), same wide travel range. Orange Juice has the open-air back deck and the iconic look. Kuttin Loose is built around being loudest and most intense inside. Different vibes — depends what you want.

**"Cheaper than Orange Juice?"**
> Yeah — $125/hr versus $135 for Orange Juice. You save $10/hr without giving up the size or travel range.
