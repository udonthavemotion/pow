## How To Quickly Pick A Bus When A Customer Is Undecided

Ask three questions:
1. **"How many people?"** (under 40 means any bus works for size; over 40 is two buses or a charter conversation)
2. **"Where you headed?"** (out of the Houma area? → Tier 1 only — Orange Juice / Kuttin Loose / Cotton Candy)
3. **"What's the event?"** (bachelorette + photos = Cotton Candy. Birthday + parade = Orange Juice. Going hard = Kuttin Loose. VIP local = Rackz.)

That's almost always enough to narrow it down to two options. From there, send photos of those two and let the customer pick.

## Universal Features (Every Bus)

Every bus in the POW fleet includes:
- Licensed, sober driver (always — you do not drive)
- Premium sound system with Bluetooth / AUX input
- LED light show inside
- Climate control (AC and heat)
- Comfortable seating
- Insurance and DOT compliance

Most buses also include:
- Onboard coolers
- Ice and water provided
- USB charging
- Privacy windows

Bus-specific features (dance floor, dance pole, open-air deck) are called out in each bus's detailed file.

## What's NOT On Any Bus

- **Alcohol** — bring your own. POW does not provide or sell alcohol.
- **Food** — bring your own snacks/meals.
- **A bathroom** — most party buses in this class don't have on-board restrooms. Driver will make reasonable stops.
- **Smoking inside** — strictly no smoking, vaping, or open flames inside the bus.

Full policy details in `13_POLICIES_RULES_SAFETY.md`.

## Why The Customer Should Care

Most party bus companies have one bus and pretend it fits every event. POW gives the customer a fleet to pick from — same driver quality, same sound and lights, but the bus actually fits the event.

A bachelorette getting on Cotton Candy looks like a different night than the same bachelorette stepping into Kuttin Loose. We don't talk customers out of the bus they want — we help them pick the one that matches the photos they want to take home.
